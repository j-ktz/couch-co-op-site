    Picks that stay honest.

    Picks for each episode open when the last one ends, so nobody picks days ahead with spoilers in hand. Pick together while you watch, or pick late for a small penalty. The app records when each pick was saved, and everyone's picks show once the episode starts.

    Your house, your rules.

    Before the season starts, members vote on the rules: team size, the pick window, what a late pick costs and more. The majority wins, the result goes into a rule book everyone can read, and the rules lock when the season starts.

    Questions people ask

    What is Fantasy Queens?

    A fantasy league app for drag watch-party groups. Your group starts a private house, everyone makes picks each episode, and a leaderboard shows who's leading after every episode.

    How do we join a house?

    Whoever starts the house shares an invite link or code. Open it on your iPhone and you're in.

    What are the ways to play?

    Weekly Call (one queen for the top, one safe, one for the bottom), Survival Bracket (rank the whole cast before the season), Team (pick a team each week) and Boot Pick (call who goes home, with a backup for a double elimination).

    When do picks lock?

    Picks for each episode open when the last one ends. Pick before or during the episode, or late for a small penalty your house sets. Everyone's picks show once the episode starts.

    Does the app handle money?

    No. If your group chips in, the House Mother can mark who's paid, but payments stay between you and your friends.

    Do I need an account?

    Yes, so your house knows it's you. Sign in with Apple, Google or email.

    Is it free?

    Yes, it's free to play. If the app offers an optional purchase, its price is shown before you buy.

    Is Fantasy Queens official?

    No. It's an unofficial fan app, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any television network, production company or performer.
