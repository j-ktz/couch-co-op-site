Support

Email support@couchcoopstudios.com. A real person reads every message.

How do picks work?

Picks for each episode open when the previous episode ends and lock when the episode starts. Your house can give a free "pick together" window after each episode, and a small penalty for picks made after it. Tap "How picks work" on any pick screen to see your house's exact times.

Why can't I pick yet?

Picks for the next episode open when the current one ends, so nobody can pick days ahead. The pick screen shows when yours opens.

How do I join a house?

Ask your House Mother for the invite code or link. In the app, tap + and choose Join with a code.

How do I change the rules?

Before the season starts, the House Mother can open a rules vote from the More menu. Members vote, and the House Mother applies the result. Once the season starts the rules are locked.

Does the app handle money?

No. If your house tracks dues, the app only records who has paid. Payments happen outside the app.

Leaving a house or deleting my account

You can leave a house at any time from its screen. To delete your account and data, use Delete account in the account menu of the app, or email support@couchcoopstudios.com from the address you signed up with, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

How do I report a photo or hide someone's?

Open Members in your house, then press and hold the person. Choose "Report photo or profile" or "Hide photo". We review reports within 24 hours.

I can't sign in

Fantasy Queens supports Sign in with Apple, Google and email with a password (at least 8 characters). If you forgot a password, email us and we'll help.
