Mackro

On the App Store

A macro tracker with a squirrel named Mack who keeps score and never judges.

  • Log a meal by describing it or snapping a photo
  • Calories and macros at a glance, with a nudge from Mack
  • Trends over 7, 30 or 90 days, plus widgets
See Mackro

TankLog

On the App Store

A free, offline journal for planted tank and shrimp keepers. Log water tests, watch the trends and know when your CO₂ drifts.

  • pH, KH, GH, nitrate and more, graded against your own ranges
  • Care schedules and dosing with reminders
  • No login, no ads. Your data stays on your device
See TankLog
