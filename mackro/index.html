Questions people ask

Is Mackro free?

Yes. Barcode scanning, food search, calories, macros, water, streaks and 7-day trends are free, with no ads. Mackro Pro is optional.

What does Mackro Pro add?

Logging by describing a meal or snapping a photo, 30 and 90-day trends with full history, weekly targets that follow your weight trend, training and rest day targets, Apple Health sync and meal templates. It's $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial, and arrives with version 2.1.

How does photo logging work?

Take a photo of your plate and Mack estimates the calories and macros in a few seconds using AI. It's an estimate, so mixed dishes can be off. You can edit any entry.

Do I need an account?

No. Mackro never asks for your name, email or a password. Your food log is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Does Mackro work with Apple Health and Apple Watch?

With Mackro Pro, Mackro can write calories, macros, water and weight to Apple Health, and you can log a meal by dictating on Apple Watch. Health data never leaves your phone.

What data does Mackro collect?

Anonymous product analytics that never include your food, health or weight data. If you use AI logging, only the description or photo you submit is sent to estimate macros. No ads and no data sales. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is Mackro medical or diet advice?

No. Targets are estimates from what you enter. For medical or diet guidance, talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian.
