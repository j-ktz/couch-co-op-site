Frequently asked questions

Does Pocketarr get shows or movies for me?

No. It's a window onto the Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr and Plex servers you already run. Your servers do the work, and anything that changes a server asks you first.

Do I need my own server?

To see your own media, yes: a Sonarr or Radarr server your iPhone can reach. To look around first, tap Try the Demo.

Can it notify me?

Pocketarr has no server of its own, so it can't notify you while it's closed. Settings has a step-by-step guide to the notifications Sonarr and Radarr can send you themselves.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time $14.99 with a 7-day free trial, and Family Sharing is included.
