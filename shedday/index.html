A care log for reptile keepers

Who ate,who shed,who's due.

Shedday is a simple care log for snakes, geckos, bearded dragons, turtles and the rest. Log a feeding in one tap, see each animal's last 28 days at a glance, and know what is due today.

One tap to log a meal.

Tap Ate or Refused on the animal's card. Shedday fills in the same prey as last time, and a short Undo covers a slip. Need a different day or prey? Tap Details.

The last 28 days, drawn like belly plates.

Each animal's strip is a row of plates, like the scales under a snake. A filled plate means it ate, an outline means it refused, a notch means it regurgitated and a pale stripe marks a shed day.

Weights as a chart.

Log grams, see the line. Weights and lengths are free to log; the charts come with Pro. Switch between grams and ounces any time.

Know what's due.

Set how often each animal needs feeding, a water change or a cleaning. Shedday counts from the last time you did it and sends one reminder when it is due.

Facts, not advice.

Days since fed

Counted from the last meal the animal actually ate. A refused meal does not reset it, and a run of refusals is shown as a plain count.

Enclosures

Log temperature and humidity for each tub or tank, and note when a UVB bulb was changed.

Your data is yours

Export every log to one CSV, or a PDF summary for each animal. Import a CSV to bring it back.

Nothing leaves your iPhone

No account, no analytics and no server. Photos and notes stay on the device.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shedday tell me how to care for my animal?

No. It is a log. It shows facts like days since fed and never says what a number means. For health questions, ask a reptile vet.

Which animals can I track?

Any reptile. The picker has 31 common species, from ball pythons and leopard geckos to tortoises, and you can type your own.

Will my data sync to my other devices?

Not yet. Everything is stored on this iPhone, so you can export a CSV any time with Pro and keep a copy.

Is it a subscription?

You choose. Pro is $14.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $24.99 once. The free plan covers two animals with no time limit.
