Privacy Policy

Short version: Shedday doesn't collect any data about you. Effective October 29, 2026.

What Shedday stores, and where

Your animals, their feedings, sheds, weights, notes, photos, enclosures, readings and care schedules are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Shedday has no server of its own and does not sync to iCloud, so we can't see any of it.

To delete an animal and everything logged for it, open the animal, tap Edit, then Delete. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that iPhone. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, your backup may include the app's data, under your own Apple Account settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Shedday has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. In the App Store's privacy labels this is listed as "Data Not Collected".

Photos

When you add a photo of an animal or to a note, you choose it with the system photo picker. Shedday receives only the photos you pick, keeps a reduced copy inside the app, and never uploads it.

Widgets

The Home Screen widgets show what is due today. To do that, Shedday keeps a small summary (animal names, task names and due dates) in storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same iPhone. It never leaves the device.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications. They're never sent from a server, and Shedday asks for permission the first time you set one up.

Purchases

Shedday Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and tells the app only whether Pro is active. We never see your payment details, name or email.

Export and sharing

The CSV file and the PDF summary are created on your iPhone and go only where you send them. Photos are not included in the CSV.

Children

Shedday doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
