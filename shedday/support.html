How do I log a meal?

On Home, tap Ate or Refused on the animal's card. Shedday fills in the same prey as last time. Tap Details to choose a different day, prey or count. A short Undo appears after a one-tap log.

What do the plates on each card mean?

The strip shows the last 28 days, oldest on the left, today on the right. A plate filled with the animal's color means it ate. An outline means it refused. A notch means it regurgitated. A pale diagonal stripe marks a shed day, and a faint plate means nothing was logged.

What does "Fed 5 days ago" count from?

From the last meal the animal actually ate. A refused meal doesn't reset it. "Refused 2 in a row" counts the newest feedings that were refused, and a meal that was eaten or regurgitated ends the count.

Why does an animal say "View only"?

The free plan covers 2 animals. If you have more, for example after a Pro subscription ended, the oldest two stay editable and the rest become view only. Nothing is ever deleted. Pro makes them all editable again.

How do reminders work?

Open an animal, tap Care, and set how often something is due. A feeding schedule counts from the newest feeding you logged, whether it was eaten or not. Other tasks count from when you tap Done today. Shedday sends one reminder when a task is due, at the time you choose in Settings. Reminders are part of Pro.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings › Notifications › Shedday on your iPhone, then the reminder time in Shedday's own Settings. A task that became due after the reminder time today gets its reminder the next morning.

Deleting an animal

Open the animal, tap Edit, then Delete. That removes the animal and everything logged for it, for good.

Exporting and importing

In Settings, under Your data, Export as CSV saves every log, schedule and enclosure in one file, and each animal has a PDF summary. Import a CSV brings a file back in, and importing the same file twice doesn't create duplicates. Photos stay on the iPhone and aren't in the CSV. Export and import are part of Pro.

Restoring Shedday Pro

On a new phone, open Settings in Shedday and tap Restore purchases. Pro follows your Apple Account.

The widget says "Open Shedday"

Open the app once after installing or updating, so the widget can find your animals. Widgets are part of Pro.

Does Shedday give care advice?

No. It's a log. It shows facts like days since fed and never tells you what a number means. For health questions, ask a reptile vet.
