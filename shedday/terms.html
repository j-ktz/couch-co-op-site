Shedday Pro

Shedday Pro unlocks unlimited animals, reminders, weight and length charts, widgets, the enclosure log, and CSV and PDF export. It's available as:

  • Yearly: $14.99 a year (or the local equivalent). New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the trial ends. The subscription renews automatically each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Lifetime: $24.99 once (or the local equivalent), for as long as Shedday is available.

If Pro ends, every animal you already have stays. The oldest two remain editable and the others become view only until Pro returns. Your data is never locked or deleted.
