Pickleball round robin guides

Plain answers to the questions organizers search for. Every schedule and table in these guides was generated by Sitout's own rotation engine, the same one the app runs.

  • Pickleball rotation for 9 players: who sits out each roundA full 9-round rotation on 2 courts where everyone sits out once and partners everyone else once. Plus 1 court, and sit-out tables for 5 to 16 players.
  • How does a pickleball round robin work?Rotating vs fixed partners, individual scoring, sit-outs, how standings are ranked, and a complete 8-player example.
  • How many players do you need for a round robin?Who plays and who sits out each round for 4 to 32 players on 1 to 8 courts.
  • Pickleball round robin for 10 players8 play and 2 sit out on 2 courts. A 10-round schedule where everyone sits out twice and plays 8 games.
  • Pickleball round robin for 12 playersEveryone plays on 3 courts with a new partner every round for 9 rounds. Plus 12 players on 2 courts.
  • Pickleball round robin for 16 playersA 15-round, 4-court rotation where every player partners every other player exactly once.
  • Mixed doubles round robinEvery man partners every woman once in 4 rounds, and what to do when the numbers are uneven.
  • How to score a pickleball round robinWin rate, point difference per game and the tiebreakers, with a worked example.
  • How to run pickleball open playPaddle stacking, challenge courts or a fair rotation, with a 13-player, 3-court example.
