Tabbed Plumber Support

Tabbed: Journeyman Plumber, exam prep for iPhone

Contact

Email support@couchcoopstudios.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Think a question is wrong?

Tap Report a problem under the question. Your report is saved in the Outbox (in About) and sent only when you choose to email or share it. Corrections ship in app updates.

Purchases

The Exam Pass is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything for both the 2021 UPC and the 2018 IPC. If you've reinstalled the app or switched phones, use Restore Purchases on the Exam Pass screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Tabbed collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
