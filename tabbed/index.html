Questions people ask

Is Tabbed for electricians or plumbers?

Both. When you first open Tabbed, you pick Electrician or Plumber. Each trade has its own questions, practice exams, progress and Exam Pass. You can switch trades later in About.

Which codes does Tabbed cover?

For electricians, the 2023 and 2026 editions of the National Electrical Code. For plumbers, the Uniform Plumbing Code (2021) and the International Plumbing Code (2018), with fuel gas questions from the International Fuel Gas Code. Your licensing board tells you which code and edition your exam uses.

Does it match my exam's format?

You pick the exam you are taking, and practice exams run in that format. Electricians set up Tabbed for their state. Texas, for example, runs as two separately scored parts. Plumbers can pick the ICC national exams, Kansas, Maryland, Montana, Nebraska, Washington or a UPC exam in the IAPMO format.

How do you know the answers are right?

Every answer cites the code section it comes from and passes a separate blind check before it ships. If you spot a problem, tap Report a problem under the explanation. Fixes ship in updates.

Do I need my own code book?

Yes. Most journeyman exams are open book, and Tabbed's lookup drills train you to find things fast in your own tabbed book. The app cites sections but doesn't reproduce code text or tables.

Does Tabbed work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection, on the job site or in the truck. Nothing leaves your phone.

How much is it?

Free to start, for both trades: 10 questions a day with explanations, the drills, a 25-question diagnostic and a 10-question sample exam. Each Exam Pass is $29.99, a one-time purchase. Electricians buy one per code edition (2023 or 2026). One Plumber Exam Pass covers both the UPC and the IPC. No subscription.

Can Tabbed guarantee I pass?

No study tool can. Passing depends on your own preparation. Tabbed shows a readiness estimate so you can see where to focus.

Is Tabbed affiliated with NFPA, IAPMO or ICC?

No. Tabbed is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or approved by NFPA, IAPMO, the International Code Council, PSI, Prometric, Pearson VUE or any licensing board.
