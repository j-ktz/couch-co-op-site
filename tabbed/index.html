Questions people ask

Which code editions does Tabbed Electrician cover?

The 2023 and 2026 editions of the National Electrical Code. Your state licensing board tells you which edition your exam uses.

Does it match my state's exam?

You set up Tabbed Electrician for your state, and practice exams run in that state's format. Texas, for example, runs as two separately scored parts.

How do you know the answers are right?

Every answer cites the code section it comes from and passes a separate blind check before it ships. If you spot a problem, tap Report a problem under the explanation. Fixes ship in updates and the release notes list them.

Do I need my own code book?

Yes. Most journeyman exams are open book, and Tabbed Electrician's lookup drills train you to find things fast in your own tabbed book. The app cites sections but doesn't reproduce code text.

Does Tabbed Electrician work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection, on the job site or in the truck.

How much is it?

Free to start: 10 questions a day with explanations, key drills, a 25-question diagnostic and a 10-question sample exam. The Exam Pass is $29.99, a one-time purchase per code edition. No subscription.

Can Tabbed Electrician guarantee I pass?

No study tool can. Passing depends on your own preparation. Tabbed Electrician shows a readiness estimate so you can see where to focus.

Is Tabbed Electrician affiliated with NFPA?

No. Tabbed Electrician is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or approved by NFPA or any licensing board.
