Tabbed Support

Tabbed, journeyman exam prep for electricians and plumbers, for iPhone

Contact

Email support@couchcoopstudios.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days. Tell us whether you're studying as an electrician or a plumber.

Electrician or plumber

When you first open Tabbed, you pick Electrician or Plumber. Each trade has its own questions, practice exams, progress and Exam Pass. You can switch trades later in About.

Think a question is wrong?

Tap Report a problem under the question. Your report is saved in the Outbox (in About) and sent only when you choose to email or share it. Corrections ship in app updates.

Purchases

Each Exam Pass is a one-time purchase. An Electrician Exam Pass covers one code edition of the NEC (2023 or 2026). The Plumber Exam Pass covers both the 2021 UPC and the 2018 IPC. If you've reinstalled the app or switched phones, use Restore Purchases on the Exam Pass screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
