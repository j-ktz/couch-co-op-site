How testing works

Testing runs through TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they launch.

Get TestFlight

Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone.

Open the link

Tap an app's TestFlight link on your iPhone, then Accept and Install.

Use it, then tell us

Use it the way you normally would. Take a screenshot in the app to send feedback, or email us.

It's free. Purchases inside a test version are test purchases, and you're never charged for them.

Leave any time. Delete the app, or open TestFlight and tap Stop Testing.

What we see. TestFlight sends us crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. Each app's privacy page covers the rest.

What we do with it. We fix what you find, and we keep a dated log of what testers told us and what changed.
