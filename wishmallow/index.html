Wish for anything.

Type a wish like "a strawberry bed" or "a sleepy cloud lamp" and it appears with a poof, ready to place. You can wish for new pals too.

Your pals live their own little lives.

Pals cook, eat, nap, watch TV, read, dance and chat with each other. Each one has moods, likes and a bedtime, and they take the stairs like anyone else.

Build it your way.

Add rooms and floors, paint the walls, lay the floors and arrange every piece. Then set your house in a meadow, on a beach, in the mountains, on a city rooftop or in Future Tokyo.

Frequently asked questions

How do wishes work?

You type a few words. They go to our wish helper, where Claude, an AI from Anthropic, picks the shape, colors and pattern from the game's own pieces. If the helper can't be reached, the game designs the piece on your phone instead.

Is it safe for kids?

Wishmallow is made for all ages. The wish helper keeps everything cute and turns down wishes that aren't, and every wished item has a Report button.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and nothing to log in to. Your house is saved on your phone.

Is it a subscription?

No. Mallow Magic is a one-time $6.99, and the free game never runs out or makes you wait.
