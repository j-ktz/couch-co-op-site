Wishmallow privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

The Short Version

  • Wishmallow has no account and no sign-up, and it never asks for your name, email or age.
  • Your house, pals and settings are saved on your iPhone.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking in the app.
  • The only thing that leaves your iPhone during play is a wish you choose to make, or a report you choose to send. You can turn wishes off in Settings.

What's Kept on Your iPhone

  • Your house: its name, rooms, walls, floors, furniture, the scene and weather you picked, and every item you wished for.
  • Your pals: their names, looks, moods and what they like.
  • Your settings, such as music, sounds, haptics and whether the wish helper is on.
  • A random install number made the first time you open the game. It isn't tied to you, your Apple Account or your device.
  • The Home Screen widget reads your pals from a shared space on the same iPhone, and a snack or pat you give from the widget is passed back to the game the same way. None of it leaves the phone.
  • Start a new house in Settings deletes your house, furniture, pals and wishes from your iPhone for good. Deleting the app removes everything.

Wishes

  • Before your first wish, Wishmallow asks for your OK to use the wish helper. You can turn it off any time in Settings, and it asks again before your next wish.
  • When you make a wish, the app sends the words you typed (up to 140 characters), your random install number and whether you have Mallow Magic to our wish helper, a small server run by Couch Co-Op Studios on Cloudflare.
  • The wish helper asks Claude, an AI from Anthropic, to design the item or pal from the game's own pieces. It sends Anthropic the wish words and a scrambled form of your install number, so Anthropic can spot abuse without knowing who you are. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, wishes are not used to train its models.
  • The wish helper does not save your wish words. Its logs keep only whether a wish worked, how long it took and how much it cost.
  • It keeps a count of how many wishes each install number made today, to enforce the daily limit. Counts are by day only.
  • If the wish helper can't be reached, the game designs the item on your iPhone instead, and nothing is sent.
  • Please don't put personal details, like a real full name or an address, in a wish.

Reports

  • Every wished item has a Report button. A report sends the item's design (its name, shape and colors), the reason you picked, any note you add (up to 500 characters) and your random install number.
  • Reports are kept for up to 180 days so we can review them and make wishes safer, then deleted automatically.

Photos

  • Photo mode can save a picture of your house to your photo library. Wishmallow asks only for permission to add photos. It can't see, read or change anything already in your library.
  • Share opens the iPhone share sheet, and you choose where the picture goes.

Purchases

  • Mallow Magic is a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Wishmallow never sees your payment details, and nothing about the purchase is sent anywhere else.

Email

  • Email support in Settings opens an email draft that you write and send yourself. We use what you send only to answer you.

Children

  • Wishmallow is made for all ages. It has no account, no chat with other players, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't ask anyone for personal information.

Who Sees What

  • We don't sell, rent or share information with anyone for advertising.
  • Cloudflare runs the wish helper's server and Anthropic designs the wishes, each only to provide that service.
  • Like any request on the internet, a wish reaches those servers from your internet address. Wishmallow doesn't store it.

Changes and Questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything new about what leaves your iPhone will be explained here before it ships. Questions or a request about a report you sent: write to support@couchcoopstudios.com.
