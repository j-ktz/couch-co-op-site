Wishmallow support

Email support@couchcoopstudios.com. A real person reads every message. In the game, Settings, Email support starts one for you.

How do I make a wish?

Open the Wish tab and type what you'd like, such as "a strawberry bed" or "a sleepy cloud lamp". The item appears with a poof, ready to place in your house. You can wish for a new pal the same way.

It says wishes need my OK

Wishes use our wish helper, which asks Claude, an AI from Anthropic, to design the item. The game asks before the first wish. To turn it on or off, open Settings and find Wishes. The privacy policy explains exactly what is sent.

I've used all my wishes for today

Free play has 3 furniture wishes and 1 pal wish a day. Mallow Magic raises that to 30 furniture wishes and 10 pal wishes. New wishes arrive each day.

My wish was turned down

Wishmallow is made for all ages, so the wish helper turns down wishes that aren't cozy or kind, and it swaps brand names for an original look. Try different words.

Something I wished for doesn't look right

Tap Report on the item, pick a reason and add a note if you like. We read every report. You can also put it away in build mode.

Do wishes work without the internet?

If the wish helper can't be reached, the game designs the item on your iPhone instead, so a wish still comes true. Online wishes are more inventive.

How do I build?

Switch from Live to Build at the top. The drawer at the bottom has furniture, storage, walls, floors and rooms. Drag to move the view, and use the buttons on the side to turn and zoom.

How do I change the scene or the weather?

Open Settings and pick a scene (Cozy Meadow, Beach, Mountains, City Rooftop or Future Tokyo) and the weather. The house moves with everything in it.

How do I take a picture of my house?

Tap the camera button to hide every control, then Save to put the picture in Photos, or Share to send it.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Wishmallow. The widget shows how a pal is doing, and you can give them a snack or a pat from it.

What is Mallow Magic?

The one-time $6.99 upgrade. It raises the limits to 3 floors, 15 rooms and 8 pals, and gives you more wishes every day. It isn't a subscription, and there are no coins, energy or timers in the game.

Restoring Mallow Magic

On a new phone, open Settings in Wishmallow and tap Restore Mallow Magic. It follows your Apple Account.

Starting over

In Settings, Start a new house deletes your rooms, furniture, pals and wishes for good and starts fresh. It can't be undone.
